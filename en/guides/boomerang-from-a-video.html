How to Make a Boomerang from Any Video on iPhone

Updated August 7, 2026·3 min read

A boomerang is the most satisfying kind of loop: a clip plays forward, then rewinds, over and over with no visible seam. You don't need Instagram — you can make one from any video already on your iPhone.

How to make a boomerang GIF

  1. Open GIF Maker and pick a video or Live Photo.
  2. Trim to a short, one-to-two-second burst of motion.
  3. Turn on the Boomerang effect (forward + reverse).
  4. Adjust the FPS for smoothness, then tap Create GIF.
  5. Save to Photos or share.

It's all processed on your device, so your clip stays private. (Boomerang is a Pro effect in GIF Maker.)

Tips for a seamless loop

  • Start and end on similar frames so the bounce feels invisible.
  • Keep it tight. Shorter clips make punchier boomerangs.
  • Match the FPS to the motion — 15–20 FPS keeps fast movement smooth.

Prefer a straight backwards effect instead? See How to reverse a video on iPhone. New to GIFs? Start with the complete guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is a boomerang?

A boomerang plays a short clip forward and then immediately backward, creating a seamless back-and-forth loop with no obvious start or end.

Do I need Instagram to make a boomerang?

No. Instagram's Boomerang records a new clip, but with GIF Maker you can turn any existing video or Live Photo into a boomerang and export it as a GIF you can share anywhere.

What clips make the best boomerangs?

Short bursts of motion with a clear back-and-forth potential — a wave, a jump, a cheers, a hair flip. One to two seconds is ideal.
