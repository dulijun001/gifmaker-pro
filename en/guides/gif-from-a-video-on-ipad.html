How to Make a GIF from a Video on iPad

Updated August 7, 2026·4 min read

The iPad's larger screen makes it one of the most comfortable places to make a GIF — you get room to trim precisely and preview the loop at a real size before exporting.

Step by step: video to GIF on iPad

  1. Open GIF Maker and tap Convert Video.
  2. Import a clip from Photos or Files.
  3. Use the wide timeline to trim to the 1–5 second moment you want.
  4. Adjust resolution and FPS if you'd like more control.
  5. Tap Create GIF, then save or share.

As on iPhone, the whole conversion happens on your device — nothing is uploaded.

Make the most of the bigger screen

  • Trim frame-by-frame. The wider timeline makes it easy to land on the exact start and end frames.
  • Preview larger. Check that the loop feels natural before you commit to the export.
  • Use Split View. Keep your source app open alongside GIF Maker to compare clips.

After you export

Your GIF is saved to Photos and to GIF Maker's library, ready for Messages, email, or any app. The settings and tips are the same across devices — see the complete guide to making GIFs on iPhone for the full rundown, or learn the ideal frame rate in What FPS should a GIF be?.

Frequently asked questions

Is making a GIF on iPad different from iPhone?

The steps are identical — the main advantage on iPad is the larger screen, which makes trimming to the exact frame easier and lets you preview the loop at a bigger size.

Can I use a video from Files on iPad?

Yes. GIF Maker can import videos from both the Photos app and the Files app, so clips you've saved from other apps work too.

Does GIF Maker work on all iPads?

It runs on any iPad with iOS/iPadOS 16.0 or later. The bigger canvas makes it especially comfortable for fine-tuning your GIF.
