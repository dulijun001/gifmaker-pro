How to Make a GIF from a Video on iPhone

Updated August 7, 2026·4 min read

A GIF is the best way to share a quick, silent, auto-looping moment — a reaction, a highlight, a funny second from a longer clip. Here's how to turn any video already on your iPhone into one.

Step by step: video to GIF

  1. Open GIF Maker and tap Convert Video.
  2. Choose a video from Photos or Files.
  3. Use the timeline to trim the exact section you want. Aim for 1–5 seconds.
  4. (Optional) Adjust speed, resolution, and FPS — see below.
  5. Tap Create GIF, then Save to Photos or share directly.

Everything runs on your device, so the clip is never uploaded and the export finishes in seconds.

Pick the right moment

The best GIFs are tightly trimmed. Before you export:

  • Cut to the single action or expression that matters.
  • Start a beat early and end a beat late so the loop doesn't feel abrupt.
  • Keep the subject centered — GIFs are often viewed small.

Tune the quality

  • Resolution controls sharpness. 480p is great for sharing; go higher for detail-heavy clips.
  • FPS controls smoothness. 15 FPS suits most videos; use 20 for fast motion.

If the file ends up too big to send, see How to reduce GIF file size on iPhone.

Save and share

Your finished GIF is added to the Photos app and to GIF Maker's library, ready to drop into iMessage, WhatsApp, or social apps.

Frequently asked questions

What video length makes the best GIF?

One to five seconds is the sweet spot. A short, tightly trimmed clip loops cleanly, stays sharp, and keeps the file small enough to send anywhere.

Can I make a GIF from a slow-motion video?

Yes. Slow-motion clips work well — you can even speed them back up in GIF Maker before exporting so the loop feels natural.

Will converting a video to a GIF lower the quality?

GIFs use fewer colors than video, so some quality change is normal. Keeping the clip short and choosing a higher resolution and FPS keeps it looking crisp.
